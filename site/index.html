Recipes prototype

Recipes in the left menu

Open the menu: Recipes is the new item (outlined). It opens a searchable list of the old NWS recipes.

Search matches dish names and ingredients ("paneer", "oats").

Meal tabs under the search: All, Breakfast, Lunch, Snacks, Dinner, Dessert. Breakfast includes On waking and Pre breakfast; Snacks covers Morning and Evening snack; Dinner includes Post dinner.

The filter button beside the search opens a sheet with Diet (Veg, Egg, Non-veg, Vegan) and Cuisine as pills; pick one or more. Reset clears them; the button says how many recipes you'll get. Once applied, the button shows how many are on, and a quiet line under the tabs says what they are, with Clear.

A recipe shows its photo, ingredients, nutrition (where the recipe has it) and method. View only.
